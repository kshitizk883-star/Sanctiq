    Questions people ask

    Is this a bank decision?

    No. Sanctiq estimates your chances. Only a lender can approve or reject a loan after verifying your documents.

    Does using it affect my CIBIL score?

    No. It never pulls your credit report. You enter your score yourself, so there is no hard enquiry.

    Which lenders does it cover?

    Fifteen major lenders: five public-sector banks, five private banks and five NBFCs. Use the filters above the tower chart to compare each group.

    Is my data stored?

    No. The calculation happens in your browser and nothing is sent or saved.

    Sanctiq is an educational estimate, not financial advice or an offer of credit. Lender names are used only to describe eligibility patterns and imply no affiliation.
